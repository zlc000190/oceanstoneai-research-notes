# Ocean Stone AI evidence provenance ledger

[Ocean Stone AI](https://oceanstoneai.org/) is the project entry point for this independent vertical-AI project pending primary-source confirmation review template. Confirm current availability, features, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Evidence boundary

For this workflow, collect public known-answer cases, adjacent citations, uncertainty labels, and dated audit evidence. A search result, directory listing, or copied marketing statement is a discovery lead rather than final evidence.

## Ledger fields

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Claim | One testable statement, written without promotional language |
| Primary source | Canonical URL, publisher, update date, and access date |
| Test evidence | Input, settings, raw result, environment, and timestamp |
| Confidence | Confirmed, provisional, contradicted, or unknown |
| Owner | Person responsible for refreshing or retiring the claim |
| Expiry | Date or event that triggers re-verification |

## Acceptance rule

Do not combine multiple claims in one row. Keep contradictory evidence, explain why one source is preferred, and label assumptions. Never place credentials, private customer records, identity documents, or unpublished media in the ledger.
