# Ocean Stone AI release-readiness evidence pack

[Ocean Stone AI](https://oceanstoneai.org/) is the project entry point for this independent vertical-AI project pending primary-source confirmation review template. Verify current availability, behavior, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Required contents

Build one versioned, reviewer-readable packet containing:

- release scope, owner, commit or artifact identifier, and intended audience;
- primary-source and policy checks with access dates;
- public known-answer cases, adjacent citations, uncertainty labels, owner notes, and dated review evidence;
- known-answer, boundary, adversarial, accessibility, and rollback results;
- unresolved risks with explicit acceptance or blocking decisions;
- data-flow, permissions, retention, licensing, and jurisdiction notes;
- monitoring thresholds, incident contact path, and retirement trigger.

## Approval rule

The reviewer must be able to reproduce critical checks from the packet without undocumented access. Record the exact version approved and any conditions. A generated file, green status badge, or successful request alone is insufficient when the output is missing, stale, inaccessible, or outside the approved scope.

Remove secrets and personal data before sharing. If evidence depends on a private system, include a redacted result and a reproducible verification procedure instead of copying restricted content.
