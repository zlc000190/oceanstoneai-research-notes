# Ocean Stone AI source, license, and rights register

[Ocean Stone AI](https://oceanstoneai.org/) is the project entry point for this independent vertical-AI project pending primary-source confirmation review template. Verify current availability, behavior, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Record every material source

Before publication or reuse, list the source owner, canonical URL, access date, license or permission basis, permitted transformations, attribution requirement, retention limit, and reviewer. Preserve public known-answer cases, adjacent citations, uncertainty labels, owner notes, and dated review evidence.

| Source class | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| User-supplied material | Is the uploader authorized, and is the intended use documented? |
| Public dataset or document | What license, version, jurisdiction, and attribution apply? |
| Model or external service | What input, output, training-use, and redistribution terms apply? |
| Generated or transformed output | Can it be published, edited, sold, or shared downstream? |

## Rights gate

Mark unknown ownership, conflicting terms, missing consent, biometric or identity data, private records, and restricted media as blocked. Do not infer permission from public visibility. Save a dated citation or redacted approval record so another reviewer can reproduce the decision.
